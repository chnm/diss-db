About the collection

A catalogue of historical scholarship—and the relationships behind it.

The History Dissertation Database brings together doctoral work in history with the advisor, committee, and institutional connections that shaped it. It is both a research tool and an evolving record of the discipline.

Collection guide

What you can find

01
Dissertation records Titles, authors, institutions, departments, completion years, abstracts, and source links.
02
Committee relationships Advisors and readers connected to the doctoral projects they helped guide.
03
Scholarly genealogies Mentor-student relationships that can be followed across generations of historians.
04
Fields and institutions Geographic and thematic emphases alongside the universities where this work was produced.

The project

Built collaboratively, maintained as public scholarship.

The database is developed and maintained by the Roy Rosenzweig Center for History and New Media at George Mason University, in collaboration with the American Historical Association. Records draw on contributed and archival data and may vary in completeness by institution and period.
